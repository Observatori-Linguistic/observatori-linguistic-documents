OUR EARLY YEARS EDUCATION At Hamelin-Laie, our Early Years students embark on a meaningful and joyful learning journey through the International Early Years Curriculum (IEYC) – a play-based, inquiry-led approach that supports their academic, personal, and social development from the very beginning. Each day is filled with engaging, hands-on experiences that spark curiosity and creativity, empowering our youngest learners to take an active role in their growth. The IEYC’s thematic units are taught across multiple subject areas and begin with dynamic entry points that capture children’s attention, encourage investigation, and build deep understanding over time. Our students set personal learning goals that inspire and challenge them in a nurturing environment where their identity is valued and celebrated.
